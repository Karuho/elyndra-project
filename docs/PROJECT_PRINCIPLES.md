# Project principles

Elyndra is designed for people who want a personal digital agent that remains under their control.

1. Local-first and useful without an internet connection.
2. Online providers may exist later, but only as an explicit opt-in mode.
3. No silent provider fallback, telemetry or background upload.
4. Memory, identity, skills and audit data belong to the user.
5. Language and image models are replaceable engines, not Elyndra's identity.
6. The deterministic core must continue working when no model is available.
7. Models used as teachers require a recorded license review and explicit approval.
8. Personal secrets must never be included in training datasets.
9. Generated training samples require provenance and review status.
10. The project is intended primarily for natural persons and accessible hardware.
